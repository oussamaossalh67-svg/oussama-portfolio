About Myself |

I'm Oussama, a Web & Graphic Designer with 1 year of experience and 40+ completed UI/UX projects. I create modern, user-focused digital experiences by combining creativity, functionality, and clean design.

Passionate about innovation, I strive to deliver impactful solutions that help brands stand out — from brand identity and social media design to UI/UX and motion work.

I trained at OFPPT in Infographie, Morocco, and keep building on that through online courses in graphic design, UI/UX, branding, motion design, and digital marketing.

Outside the tools, I bring creative thinking, strong communication, teamwork, and a close attention to detail to every project I take on.
